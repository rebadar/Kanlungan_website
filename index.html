Kanlungan: Your Digital Sanctuary

An Anonymous Teletherapy Mobile Application for Mental Health Support

Welcome to Kanlungan, a multi-platform digital ecosystem developed to provide accessible, confidential, and professional emotional support for the modern age.

Start Your Journey

The Crisis We Are Solving

In a world where mental well-being is paramount, we recognize that the greatest barriers to care are often social stigma and the severe shortage of qualified professionals.

Kanlungan (a Filipino term meaning "sanctuary" or "refuge") is your secure, anonymous digital safe space, connecting you to licensed mental health professionals and comprehensive self-help tools, all designed to promote long-term emotional resilience and stability.

Our Features

Kanlungan is a comprehensive system built on three integrated platforms: the Mobile Application for Users, and Web Platforms for Professionals and Administrators.

I. The Mobile Application (For Users)

Anonymous Teletherapy

Secure, real-time audio and video calls connecting you directly with licensed mental health professionals for confidential one-on-one sessions (Paid Service).

Digital Safe Space

Join a secure, moderated online Community Support forum for peer discussions and connect with others who understand your journey.

24/7 Crisis Companion

Access a complimentary AI Chatbot that provides supportive conversations, coping tips, and resource suggestions at any time of day.

Self-Help Toolkit

A library of free resources, including soothing calming music, inspirational quotes/mantras, and audiobooks to help manage stress and anxiety.

Progress Tracker

Monitor your well-being through essential self-management tools such as journaling, symptom tracking, medication tracking, and habit tracking.

Emergency Response

A dedicated Emergency Crisis Button that automatically displays for high-risk conditions and prompts an immediate call to emergency services.

Mobile Application Screenshots

II. The Web Platform (For Professionals & Admin)

For Mental Health Professionals

Therapist Dashboard, Session Management tools, Patient Record Access, and Community Forum Management to supervise the safe-space environment.

For Administrators

Psychologist Verification, System Oversight, and Financial and Payment Oversight to ensure platform integrity and compliance.

Our Technology

Kanlungan was developed using a modern, scalable, and secure technology stack, prioritizing user privacy and a seamless experience. The project was built using the Agile Scrum framework to ensure continuous feature testing and refinement.

Category Technology/Tool Used Rationale
Framework & Language Flutter (Framework) and Dart (Language) Enables multi-platform development for a consistent user experience on both web and mobile.
Database & Backend Firebase (Firestore) Provides a reliable, scalable cloud infrastructure for secure data storage.
Financial Integration PayMongo Used for secure management of paid services and transactions.
Security & Compliance Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 Strict adherence to legal and ethical compliance, ensuring user consent and data minimization.
Development Tools Visual Studio Code, Android Studio, Git Standard industry tools for efficient coding, debugging, and version control.

Powering Confidential Care

Our technology stack ensures that your data is protected with enterprise-grade security while providing a seamless experience across all devices.

The application follows the Agile Scrum framework for continuous improvement and feature refinement based on user feedback.
